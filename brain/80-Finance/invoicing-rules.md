# Invoicing & unit economics

1. **Unit Economics Formula**:
   - Retail Price: $49.99 USD
   - Product Sourcing (CJ Dropshipping / Zendrop) + Air Shipping: -$14.00 USD
   - Target Customer Acquisition Cost (Meta/TikTok Ads): -$16.00 USD
   - Stripe / PayPal Processing (2.9% + $0.30): -$1.75 USD
   - Net Profit Per Unit: **$18.24 USD (~36.5% Net Margin)**
2. **Payouts & Currency Settlement**: All foreign transactions processed in USD/EUR through Stripe India. Automatic daily/weekly settlements into Indian Bank Account via RBI export compliance.
3. **Dispute & Chargeback Defense**: Maintain dispute rate below 0.75%. Submit proof of delivery and tracking numbers automatically on any Stripe claim.
