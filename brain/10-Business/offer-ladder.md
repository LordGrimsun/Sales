# Offer ladder

1. **Everyday Tops, Shirts & Accessories ($25 – $45)**: Fast impulse checkout (Polka-dot ethnic printed shirts, graceful satin OL business tops, rolling perfumes).
2. **Signature Dresses, Knits & Co-ord Sets ($45 – $79)**: Core catalog drivers with high margins (Split knitted maxi dresses, thick pullover sweaters, 2-piece wide leg pants suits, off-shoulder evening gowns).
3. **Outerwear & Deluxe Statement Bundles ($89 – $149+)**: Ruffled pleated blazers, double-breasted splicing coats, faux suede motorcycle jackets, and mix-and-match outfit bundles.
