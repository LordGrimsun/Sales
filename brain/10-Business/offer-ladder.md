# Offer ladder

1. **Impulse Buys & Viral TikTok Problem Solvers ($29 – $49)**: Fast impulse checkout, solves an immediate nuisance (car cleaning gel, anti-fog mirrors, posture correctors, pet grooming hair erasers).
2. **Signature Lifestyle & Wellness Innovations ($49 – $89)**: High perceived value, transformative benefits (red light facial therapy wands, orthopedic memory foam contour pillows, ultrasonic jewelry & glasses cleaners, ambient sunset lamps).
3. **High-Ticket Bundles & Deluxe Kits ($99 – $199+)**: Multi-pack bundles (Buy 2 Get 1 Free, Complete Home Set), high AOV (Average Order Value) driver to absorb customer acquisition costs (CAC).

Every product page includes 1-click upsells and post-purchase discounts. Product descriptions follow [[proposal-template]]. Unit margin economics in [[business-model]].
