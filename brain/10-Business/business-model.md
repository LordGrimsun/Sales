# Business model

Sunnyeora is a high-margin global fashion & lifestyle e-commerce brand selling curated women's dresses, luxury knitwear, coordinated 2-piece sets, elegant outerwear blazers, and versatile everyday styling to customers in the US, UK, Canada, Australia, and Europe.
All customer orders are paid in USD via our Shopify storefront (https://sunnyeora.myshopify.com), and fulfilled directly to foreign customers via verified dropshipping partners (Dropshipman / CJ Dropshipping) in 7–10 days with trackable USPS/Royal Mail air shipping.
