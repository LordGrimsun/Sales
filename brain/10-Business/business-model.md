# Business model

Sunnyeora is a high-margin global e-commerce and cross-border dropshipping brand headquartered in New Delhi, India, selling viral, high-demand lifestyle products, home innovations, wellness gadgets, and everyday problem-solvers to customers in the US, UK, Canada, Australia, and Europe.
All customer orders are paid in USD via Stripe and PayPal on our Shopify storefront, and fulfilled directly to foreign customers via CJ Dropshipping and Zendrop in 7–10 days with trackable USPS/YunExpress shipping.
