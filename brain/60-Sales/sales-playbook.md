# Sales playbook & product validation

Winning product criteria for Sunnyeora:
1. **Solves a Pain or Has High 'Wow' Factor**: Product must dramatically solve a problem (posture, pain, cleaning, sleep) OR trigger immediate visual amazement in a 3-second video.
2. **High Perceived Value & Margins**: Sourcing price on CJ Dropshipping / AliExpress must be < $15 so we can retail at $49 to $79 (65%+ gross margin).
3. **Not Readily Available in Local Stores**: Avoid generic items sold at Target or Walmart.
4. **Lightweight & Durable**: Package weight under 1.2 kg to ensure fast, cheap air shipping ($4–$8 per unit) without transit breakage.
5. **Customer Objections Handled On-Page**:
   - *Shipping time*: "Ships via USPS Priority with real-time tracking (7–10 business days)."
   - *Trust/Legitimacy*: "30-Day Money Back Guarantee, SSL 256-bit encrypted checkout with Stripe/PayPal."
   - *Returns*: "Hassle-free replacement policy if damaged or defective."
