# Compliance checklist & export rules

1. **Store Legal Policies**: GDPR/CCPA-compliant Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, Refund Policy (30 days), and Shipping Policy clearly visible in Shopify footer.
2. **India Export Compliance**: DGFT IEC (Import Export Code) registered for cross-border services, automatic FIRC/e-BRC documentation from bank upon Stripe INR remittances.
3. **Supplier Quality Verification**: Blind test orders before scaling any product to verify packaging, absence of Chinese marketing inserts, and actual shipping transit days. Checked quarterly and logged in [[log]].
