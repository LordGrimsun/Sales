# Ad playbook

1. **Testing Campaign**: Advantage+ Shopping Campaign (ASC) on Meta or CBO with 3–5 ad sets, $30–$50/day per product. Target Tier-1 countries: United States, United Kingdom, Canada, Australia.
2. **Creative Angles (3 angles per product)**:
   - **Angle 1 (The Problem / Pain Hook)**: "If you sit at a desk all day, your neck is begging for this..."
   - **Angle 2 (The Demonstration / Wow Factor)**: Instant visual before & after demonstration.
   - **Angle 3 (Social Proof / Review)**: "Why 14,000+ people switched to Sunnyeora this month."
3. **KPIs & Scaling**: Target ROAS >= 2.5x. Kill any ad set with CPC > $1.50 or zero sales after 2x break-even CAC spend. Scale winners by 20% daily budget increases or horizontal duplicate into new lookalikes. Creative follows [[visual-identity]]. Weekly metrics reported to [[MOC-Operations]].
