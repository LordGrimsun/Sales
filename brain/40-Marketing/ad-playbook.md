# Ad playbook

1. **Testing Campaign**: Advantage+ Shopping Campaign (ASC) on Meta (Facebook & Instagram) with 3–5 ad sets, $30–$50/day per product collection (Dresses, Knitwear, 2-Piece Sets). Target Tier-1 countries: United States, United Kingdom, Canada, Australia.
2. **Creative Angles (3 angles per collection)**:
   - **Angle 1 (The Boutique Look for Less)**: "Looks like a $180 boutique piece, costs under $49..."
   - **Angle 2 (Effortless Styling / GRWM)**: "Get ready with me in 60 seconds — this 2-piece set does all the styling for you."
   - **Angle 3 (Seasonal Transition / Comfort)**: "The cozy knit pullover sweater you will live in all winter."
3. **KPIs & Scaling**: Target ROAS >= 2.5x. Kill any ad set with CPC > $1.40 or zero sales after 2x break-even spend. Scale winners by 20% daily budget increases or horizontal duplicate into lookalike audiences. Creative follows [[visual-identity]]. Weekly metrics reported to [[MOC-Operations]].
